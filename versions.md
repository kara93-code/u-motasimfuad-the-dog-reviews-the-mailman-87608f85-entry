# The Dog Reviews the Mailman — 3 versions

Gate: 1–200 karakter, satu baris, UTF-8. Juri: suara anjing,

## Versi A (124 karakter) — provisional di main.ts

> One star. He rang twice, left a parcel for the neighbours, and still wanted a wag. I barked. He ran. Five stars for the run.

Kenapa: detail kunjungan paling spesifik (bunyi bel dua kali,
paket buat tetangga, minta kibasan ekor), dendam anjing jelas,
rating berbalik di akhir — bintang lima bukan buat kirimannya,
tapi buat larinya. Tiga ketukan: keluhan, gonggongan, pembalikan.

## Versi B (110 karakter)

> Five stars. The mailman brings letters, leaves no treats, and still runs when I bark. I have trained him well.

Kenapa: suara anjing paling percaya diri — tukang pos yang
lari tiap digonggong itu hasil "latihan" si anjing. Suap yang
tidak pernah datang (no treats) jadi mesin keluhan, punchline
di empat kata terakhir.

## Versi C (92 karakter)

> One star. He promised a treat, gave me a bill, and ran. I kept the bill. I ate the envelope.

Kenapa: paling pendek dan paling bisa dikutip, janji suap yang
dikhianati (treat jadi tagihan), lalu balas dendam konkret:
tagihan disimpan, amplopnya dimakan. Twist di kata terakhir.
